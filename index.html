My brain runs on ADHD and never closes a single tab.

Ideas arrive the way cats do - all at once, at three in the morning, and utterly convinced it’s urgent. So I let them out: stories and essays in German and English, songs of my own, pictures that sometimes refuse to behave, and little tools that make the chaos a bit tidier.

Making things is how I clear the room in my head. Whatever you find here is what came out.

Writer, songwriter, image-maker. Trans, a little chaotic, and very much here. Come in, have a look around.
