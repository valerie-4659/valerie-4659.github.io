I'm Poly, not a slut

10 June 20264 min read

I love many people. And yes, some of them are maybe you ❤️

I flirt a lot. Not because I am careless, not because nothing means anything to me, but because I love that slow, dangerous little moment when a conversation opens up. When two people start teasing, trusting, revealing pieces of themselves. When a hot flirt suddenly becomes something warmer. Butterflies. That soft chaos in your chest. That ridiculous glow your brain gives you when it decides: oh no, this person matters.

I love love. And I know some people will look at that and think: "Well, she is just a slut"

Because I flirt openly. Because I flirt with more than one person. Because I do not pretend that my heart has only one tiny reserved seat and everyone else has to stand outside in the rain.

But that is not how I feel love.

At some point I stopped believing that love is something finite. We are told so often that there is "the one", that one perfect person who is supposed to be everything. But we already love in many directions. We love friends, family, pets, passions, memories, places, sometimes even work, even if work definitely tests that relationship hard some days.

Love is not a fuel tank. It does not become empty because you gave some of it away. Love is connection. A deep feeling of closeness. Something that can make you shine when it is present, and ache when it is missing. It has no weight, no fixed size, no inventory limit.

And honestly, how many relationships break because people expect one person to be everything? The perfect lover, best friend, emotional home, adventure partner, safety net, fantasy, routine, chaos and calm all at once. Then, months or years later, the flaws become louder than the beauty. People start resenting each other because the few painful parts cover everything that was once good.

I am not saying polyamory is better than monogamy. It is not a higher level. It is not for everyone. Monogamy can be beautiful, deep and real.

But for me, love is not ownership.

You do not own a person. You are gifted time with them. Attention. Tenderness. Trust. Moments. And that makes it more precious, not less.

To love this way, I had to let go of the idea that someone choosing another person means they are taking something away from me. I had to know my own worth. Because if your entire value depends on being the only one, jealousy will eat you alive.

As a poly person, I cannot ask my partners to be monogamous for me while I am not monogamous for them. I do not want that. I want them to flirt, to play, to feel wanted, to explore, to sext, to fall into moments, maybe even into beds, if that is what feels right for them.

Same freedom, honesty, rules.

The only exception for me is when I enter a Mistress/sub dynamic. As a sub, I belong to one Mistress in that role. I focus on her. She should know that in that space, I am hers. That is part of the game, part of the devotion, part of the delicious little madness.

But outside of that dynamic? I am poly.

Online love is real too.

Love does not always need skin against skin. It does not always need a shared couch, a shared bed, or the same city. Some people are separated by distance. Some by health. Some by life. Some use personas because it gives them the courage to feel, explore and express something they cannot safely live anywhere else.

So if we love each other, know this: I may love others too. I may flirt with them. I may have fun with them. I may care deeply about them.

You do not get me exclusively, locked away in a little emotional display case.

But what you do get is real.

When I say I love you, I mean it. When someone matters to me, they matter. I care, show up, hold space, listen, worry, smile like an idiot when they message me.

I am not a slut because my heart is open.
I am poly.

And my love is not smaller because I share it ❤️💜
