Why I'm on X

24 May 20264 min read

Back when I was still mostly making SFW images, my plan was pretty simple: grow big, collect followers, maybe one day make money with my pictures. A lot of it was about numbers. Likes, reach, followers, growth. Little counters that had to go up so you could feel good for a moment. And when they did not, you just became unhappy.

Back then I was still a man. Or at least I was trying to be one. I did not have much confidence, I never really wanted to be in the spotlight, and people often scared me. So a lot of what I did online felt very mechanical. Post images, watch numbers, analyze, continue. It was anonymous, distant, and somehow safe. But also empty.

Over time, a lot changed. The man became a woman, and Valerie became more than just a persona. She became a part of me that was finally allowed to breathe. 🌸 And since then, different things matter to me.

I became more open. Sometimes more direct. More confident. I know better now what I want, what I like, and what I no longer feel ashamed of. Not praise, not attention, not my kinks, not my lust, and not the fact that I enjoy touching people - with images, words, fantasies, or sometimes simply with honest closeness.

Yes, I like making NSFW. And of course likes and followers are nice. We can all pretend we are above that little dopamine circus, but let us be honest: when a number goes up, the brain purrs for a second like a very pleased cat. 🐈‍⬛

But that is no longer the real reason.

What matters to me is making you feel something with my images. To stir something in you. To turn you on. To pull you into your own little head cinema so deeply that everyday life becomes quieter for a moment. Maybe even so much that your hand starts wandering all by itself. I provide the fuel for your spicy imagination - and yes, I enjoy that. 🔥

But the best part is not the like. Not the repost. Not the number under an image.

The best part is when a chat opens.

Maybe it is just a quick "Hi, your images are amazing." Maybe that is all it becomes. Or maybe it turns into a real conversation. Getting to know each other. A friendship. Maybe it becomes more intimate. Maybe feelings enter the room. Maybe a tiny moment becomes something nobody expected.

And that is exactly what I love about it. 💜

I truly enjoy making images. But the encounters behind them are the real reward for me. Those little hidden layers. The things that happen when people suddenly become softer, when they open up, when they show something they usually keep hidden. When a profile picture becomes a real person.

If you write to me, there is something you should know: I live my feelings as they happen. I am direct. Sometimes very direct. If I like someone, I say so. If someone turns me on, I might say that too. And yes, sometimes I feel something that feels like love.

I am poly. I like the feeling of being in love. I think it is beautiful, intense, and alive. But I do not expect anything in return. It only matters to me that the person on the other side knows where I am emotionally. No trap, no claim, no contract with invisible fine print. Just honesty. ✨

If you cannot deal with that, that is completely okay. Then maybe you just should not chat too deeply with me.

What I do find exhausting, though, is when someone drops into my DMs with "be my mistress."

Sweetheart, breathe for a second. 😌

Start by talking to me like I am a normal person. Everything else either happens or it does not. Whether I am someone’s mistress is not a role you apply for with a form. It is a matter of sympathy, trust, chemistry, and effort. Dominance is not a fast-food menu.

So, why am I on X?

Not because of follower counts. Not because of likes. They are nice, sure. But they are not the heart of it.

I am here because of you. Because of the conversations. Because of the tension between two people. Because of those small moments where images turn into encounters. Because of what happens between the lines. Because of the tingling feeling, the curiosity, the openness, and sometimes also because of the chaos that feelings tend to bring with them. 🖤

I love watching things unfold. Watching people become more relaxed. Watching a like turn into a conversation. And watching a conversation maybe become something nobody could have planned.

That is why I am here. 💋
